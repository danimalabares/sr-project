# Rational two-jet: exact smoothness certificate

Let $I_\Delta=(f_0,\ldots,f_{15})\subset
\mathbf Q[x_1,\ldots,x_8]$ be the Stanley--Reisner ideal of the Grünbaum
sphere.  The file
[`universal_2jet_QQ.txt`](universal_2jet_QQ.txt) records exact cubics
$g_i,h_i$ for which

\[
 F_i=f_i+s g_i+s^2h_i+O(s^3)
\]

is the pullback of Macaulay2's characteristic-zero versal family along the
rational direction constructed in
[`../all-ones-versal-arc/`](../all-ones-versal-arc/).  The comparison there
also proves that the historical second-order lift induces $v_2=0$ and has
this same universal two-jet up to an infinitesimal coordinate change.

## The theorem proved here

**Two-jet smoothing theorem.**  Let
$\mathcal X\subset\mathbf P^7_{\mathbf Q[[s]]}$ be any flat formal embedded
deformation whose sixteen equations have the displayed two-jet.  Then its
geometric generic fibre is smooth.  In particular, arbitrary terms of order
$s^3$ and higher cannot change this conclusion.

This result is independent of the existence of an all-orders extension.  The
AQ bracket and Bianchi argument in the adjacent proof directory supplies one
such extension.

## The finite incidence certificate

Fix the projective chart $x_v=1$, with affine coordinates
$z_1,\ldots,z_7$.  A fibre has pure dimension three, so a point is singular
exactly when the $7\times16$ affine Jacobian $J$ has rank at most three.
Equivalently, the left kernel of $J$ contains a four-plane.

The Grassmannian $\operatorname{Gr}(4,7)$ is covered by its 35 standard
charts.  On the chart indexed by a four-subset $P\subset\{1,\ldots,7\}$,
write its universal $7\times4$ matrix as $K_P$: its rows in $P$ form
the identity and its other entries are twelve affine variables.  The
singular-incidence scheme on the pair of charts $(v,P)$ is defined by

\[
 F_0,\ldots,F_{15},\qquad J^{\mathsf T}K_P.
\]

There are 16 family equations and 64 kernel equations, hence 80 generators.
Put

\[
 A=\mathbf Q[z_1,\ldots,z_7,a_1,\ldots,a_{12}].
\]

For each generator write
$q=q_0+s q_1+s^2q_2\pmod {s^3}$.  Multiplication by arbitrary elements of
$A[s]/(s^3)$ is represented exactly by the $A$-submodule of $A^3$
generated by

\[
 (q_0,q_1,q_2),\quad(0,q_0,q_1),\quad(0,0,q_0).
\]

Thus every chart pair gives a $3\times240$ module matrix.  The exact
Macaulay2 calculation proves

\[
 (0,0,1)^{\mathsf T}\in M_{v,P}
\]

for all $8\cdot35=280$ pairs.  Equivalently,

\[
 s^2\in
 (F_i,(J^{\mathsf T}K_P)_{ij},s^3)
\]

on every incidence chart.  Of the 280 rational computations, 277 reduce at
the degree-six partial Gröbner basis; three require degree eight.  A zero
remainder against a partial basis is already an exact membership proof,
because every computed basis element is an exact combination of the input
columns.  The optional `VERIFY_LIFT=1` mode retains Macaulay2's change matrix
and directly asserts the resulting identity in the original 240 columns.

## Why this excludes every higher-order singularity

Suppose an arbitrary all-orders continuation had a geometric generic
singular point.  After a finite extension of $\mathbf Q((s))$, properness
of $\mathbf P^7$ extends the point over the corresponding DVR.  Scale its
homogeneous coordinates so that one $x_v$ is a unit; the seven affine
coordinates are then integral.  Its four-dimensional Jacobian-kernel
subspace gives a point of $\operatorname{Gr}(4,7)$.  Properness of the
Grassmannian extends that point too, and some Plücker coordinate is a unit,
so its twelve coordinates on one standard chart are integral.

For the actual continuation, every incidence generator is

\[
 q'=q+s^3r,
\]

because differentiating with respect to an affine coordinate preserves the
$s$-adic order.  Evaluate the certified identity at the integral incidence
section, where all $q'$ vanish.  It gives

\[
 s^2=s^3c
\]

for an integral $c$, an immediate valuation contradiction.  Hence no
geometric generic singular point exists.  This argument uses only the
displayed two-jet and is genuinely uniform in every $O(s^3)$ term.

The rank criterion above applies because the embedded formal deformation is
flat and the Stanley--Reisner special fibre is connected, Cohen--Macaulay,
and pure of dimension three.  Flatness makes $s$ a nonzerodivisor, so the
local rings of the total space along the special fibre, and hence their
generic-fibre localizations, are Cohen--Macaulay.  Upper semicontinuity from
the special value $h^0(\mathcal O)=1$, followed by field base change, gives
$h^0(\mathcal O)=1$ on the geometric generic fibre, so it is connected.  A
finite-type Cohen--Macaulay scheme over a field is locally
equidimensional; connectedness and the fixed degree-three Hilbert polynomial
therefore make this fibre pure of dimension three.  On a seven-variable
affine chart, regularity is then equivalent to Jacobian rank four.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, with Macaulay2 1.20 and Python 3:

```sh
python3 proofs/rational-two-jet-smoothness/verify_grassmann_all.py
```

The driver runs all 280 chart pairs exactly over $\mathbf Q$, retries a
degree-six inconclusive result at degree eight, performs one explicit
change-matrix lift check, and writes
[`certificates/grassmann_mod_s3_QQ.txt`](certificates/grassmann_mod_s3_QQ.txt).
The retained exact lift transcript is
[`certificates/grassmann_lift_spot_QQ.txt`](certificates/grassmann_lift_spot_QQ.txt).
For one chart pair, run for example

```sh
X_CHART=1 GRASSMANN_CHART=1 DEGREE_LIMIT=6 \
  M2 --script proofs/rational-two-jet-smoothness/verify_grassmann_mod_s3.m2
```

## Independent Newton cross-check

[`verify_local_smoothness.sage`](verify_local_smoothness.sage) performs the
older ridge, edge, vertex, and Newton-initial calculations for the fixed
two-jet.  Those exact local calculations are useful cross-checks, but their
former face-flag argument did not by itself control arbitrary $O(s^3)$
terms after successive weight refinements.  They are not used for the
two-jet theorem above; the Grassmann-incidence membership is the exhaustive
higher-order-robust certificate.  The reference output is retained as
[`certificates/newton_crosscheck_QQ.txt`](certificates/newton_crosscheck_QQ.txt).
